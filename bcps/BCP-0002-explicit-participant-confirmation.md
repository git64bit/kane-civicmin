## Practice

Civic Infrastructure should preserve clear evidence that participation and consequential actions are voluntary and affirmatively chosen.

Current explicit evidence includes:

- the Self-Addressed Stamped Envelope used for Owner Operator onboarding, including explicit permission to publish Participant-selected files to IPFS;
- manual installation by the Participant of the Kane County Civic Infrastructure SSL browser certificate;
- explicit in-product acknowledgement before high-attention Civic actions, beginning with `water-ants` / Publish File.

Additional explicit confirmations may be added as the system evolves.

These confirmations are evidence of participant intent. They do not replace downstream authorization, Civic policy, or applicable legal requirements.
